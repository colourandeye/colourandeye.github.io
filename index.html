OxVis

Oxford Vision Group

OxVis brings together researchers across Oxford who are interested in vision. We meet twice a year to share ideas, skills and approaches across disciplines — from ophthalmology and neuroscience to psychology, zoology and computer science.

Meetings are held in the Harold Lee Room at Pembroke College or at the Nuffield Department of Clinical Neurosciences at the John Radcliffe Hospital, and typically run from 2–5 pm with a wine reception afterwards.
