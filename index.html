About

This symposium brings together work on how vision is shaped by the environment, how it guides action, and how internal brain state influences perception and rehabilitation.

Ecology of vision

Colour signals, camouflage, underwater optics, and visual constraints in natural environments.

Vision and action

Material perception, eye movements, flight, and collision avoidance as parts of active vision.

Vision and brain states

Sleep, visual impairment, stroke, binocular vision, and plasticity across the lifespan.
